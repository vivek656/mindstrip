# Functionality: Brain Dump Ingestion

## Overview
Allows users to offload messy, disorganized thoughts without friction or formatting constraints. Accepts raw stream-of-consciousness text, simulated or real voice audio input, or one-click realistic domain presets.

## Key Capabilities
1. **Raw Text Stream-of-Consciousness:**
   - Freeform text area with zero syntax constraints.
   - Character count and typing flow with auto-expanding input.
2. **Voice Audio Dictation Simulation / Web Speech API:**
   - Instant voice input recording simulation allowing users to speak their raw thoughts aloud.
   - Real-time transcription into the brain dump staging area.
3. **One-Click Firsthand Domain Presets:**
   - **Healthcare & Caregiver:** Dialysis clinic routines, fluid restrictions, lab tracking fatigue.
   - **Engineering & Architecture:** The 2 AM refactor trap (distributed locks vs Kafka outbox pattern).
   - **Founder & Startup:** Launch day panic (Stripe webhooks, investor TAM models, button padding obsession).
   - **Personal & ADHD:** Executive dysfunction, task paralysis, dopamine chasing.
   - **Executive & Communication:** Tough 1-on-1 conversations and constructive feedback.
4. **Context & Tone Selectors:**
   - Art style selection (Newspaper, Cyberpunk Noir, Modern Webcomic, Manga, Minimalist).
   - Perspective lens toggle (Empathetic, Stoic, Satirical, Pragmatic Engineer, Candid Friend).
