# Functionality: Export and Sharing

## Overview
Allows users to save, archive, and share their synthesized comic strips and action plans.

## Key Capabilities
1. **High-Resolution Image Download (PNG):**
   - Utilizes `html-to-image` to capture the entire rendered comic strip canvas with pixel-perfect resolution.
   - Preserves comic borders, speech balloons, and typography.
2. **Social & Team Share Card:**
   - Formatted for easy sharing on Twitter/X, Discord, Slack channels, or LinkedIn.
3. **Visual Journal Archive:**
   - Option to store strips in browser localStorage for offline retrieval and personal reflection tracking over time.
4. **Print-Friendly Format:**
   - Optimized black-and-white or full-color printable layouts for physical desk pinning.
