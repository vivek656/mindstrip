# Functionality: Comic Strip Synthesis

## Overview
Transforms the cognitive breakdown into an episodic, narrative 3-to-4 panel visual comic strip. Powered by Meta Muse Wonder, Muse Image, and dynamic visual composition.

## Key Capabilities
1. **Pacing and Narrative Arc:**
   - **Panel 1 (The Trigger / Overwhelm):** Depicts the user caught in the chaos, surrounded by swirling papers, error logs, or ringing phones.
   - **Panel 2 (The Escalation / Antagonist Appears):** The personified distortion looms large, exaggerating the catastrophic outcome.
   - **Panel 3 (The Puncture / The Twist):** A reality-check moment, humorous counter-observation, or calm intervention that deflates the panic.
   - **Panel 4 (The Clarity / Step Forward):** The dust settles, a simple path forward is visible, and the protagonist takes a deep breath.
2. **Visual Art Styles:**
   - **Newspaper Classic:** Nostalgic warm tones, clean vintage linework, classic comic font.
   - **Cyberpunk Noir:** High contrast, neon accents, dystopian tech vibes.
   - **Modern Webcomic:** Friendly vibrant flat vector style, expressive anime-inspired chibi eyes.
   - **Manga Zen:** Dynamic screentones, dramatic emotion lines, black-and-white ink.
   - **Minimalist Line Art:** Crisp monochrome elegance, high whitespace.
3. **Comic Typography & Accents:**
   - Dynamic speech balloons with pointer tails.
   - Thought clouds for internal monologues.
   - Comic sound effect stickers (*ZOINK!*, *OVERHEAT!*, *PING!*, *FACEPALM*).
   - Editable dialogue text directly on the canvas.
