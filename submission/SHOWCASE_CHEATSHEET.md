# 💡 MindStrip — Showcase Cheatsheet & Demo Tips

Keep this open during your live demo or pitch to quickly reference the top talking points.

---

## 🌟 The 6 "Showstopper" Features to Flex

1. **Meta Muse Spark 1.3 Cognitive Distillation:**
   - Translates raw unstructured thoughts into 4 sequential comic beats (Setup, Escalation, Twist, Resolution).
   - Identifies cognitive distortions (Catastrophizing, Fortune Telling, Black & White Thinking).

2. **Cognitive Action Blueprint & Burden Gauge:**
   - Visual gauge displaying burden shift (e.g., `88% ➔ 18%`).
   - 3-step micro-actions under 5 minutes with real `canvas-confetti` fireworks.
   - 1-click Markdown copy for Notion, Todoist, and Apple Notes.

3. **Meta SAM 3.1 Point-and-Click Dialogue Editing:**
   - Real-time segmentation masks highlighting characters, bubbles, and props.
   - Clicking any speech bubble opens an inline dialogue editor to rewrite text on the canvas.

4. **1-Click Philosophical Lens Reframe:**
   - `🏛️ Stoic`: Marcus Aurelius dichotomy of control.
   - `🎭 Satirical`: Standup comedy / therapeutic satire.
   - `⚙️ 10x Engineer`: Root cause analysis & binary tradeoffs.
   - `🌱 Empathetic`: Gentle self-compassion.

5. **Motion Comic Video Engine & Meta Movie Gen:**
   - Dynamic Ken Burns camera tracking zooms across panel beats.
   - Built-in Web Audio API dual-oscillator ambient synth (no external audio assets needed).
   - 16:9 Widescreen & 9:16 Vertical Reel toggle.
   - Client-side `canvas.captureStream(30)` MediaRecorder `.webm` export.

6. **Apple Intelligence Minimalist Mobile Dock:**
   - Five serene tab buttons: `✦ AI Chat`, `📖 World`, `☑ Plan`, `🎬 Reel`, `⊞ Controls`.
   - Never hides features on mobile; full AI Co-Director studio is one tap away.

---

## 🎨 Best Visual Settings for Presentation

- **Theme:** Switch to **Cinema Theme** (Click the 🌙 Moon icon on the top navigation bar). High-contrast dark violet aesthetics look much crisper in recordings.
- **Pre-populated Worlds:** If you don't want to wait for live image generation during a 2-minute pitch, use an existing populated universe from the **"Continue a World"** picker to show instant, fully-rendered pages.
- **Audio:** Turn on computer sound during the Motion Reel demo to let judges hear the built-in ambient cinematic drone synthesized via the Web Audio API.
