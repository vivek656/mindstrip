## 🎙️ Spoken Script & Screen Actions

### Act 1: The Problem Hook (0:00 - 0:25)
* **Screen:** Open `http://localhost:4040`. Clean landing page with "Start Your World" and "Continue a World". Toggle Cinema Mode (🌙) for dark aesthetics.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Every knowledge worker, founder, and creator knows the feeling of a 2:00 AM mental spiral—overthinking a presentation, catastrophizing a bug, or paralyzed by decisions. Traditional note apps make you write more text and read more text. But human brains process visual narratives sixty-thousand times faster than prose.*  
  > *This is MindStrip: an AI platform built with Meta's frontier models that turns cognitive overload into serialized comic universes and actionable clarity."*

---

### Act 2: Brain Dump to Comic Page (0:25 - 0:55)
* **Screen:** Click **"Start Your World"**. Enter a vivid brain dump:  
  *"Tomorrow is our investor demo. I'm terrified our pipeline will crash, I've rewritten the pitch deck 14 times, and I can't decide whether to sleep or refactor the backend."*  
  Hit **Generate Comic Episode**.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"In under thirty seconds, our Multi-Agent Showrunner powered by Meta Muse Spark 1.3 deconstructs the cognitive distortion, scripts a four-beat dramatic arc, and feeds it to Meta Muse Image. The result is a publication-quality graphic novel spread with expressive characters, authentic speech bubbles, and sound effects—turning a paralyzing dread into an externalized narrative you can laugh at."*

---

### Act 3: Action Blueprint & Cognitive Relief (0:55 - 1:20)
* **Screen:** Scroll down or click the `[ ✓ Action Plan ]` toolbar pill. Show the Burden Shift gauge (88% ➔ 18%), read the Antagonist card ("The Perfectionist Gremlin"), and click to check off the 3 micro-actions (watch confetti blast). Click **"Copy Plan"**.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"MindStrip doesn't stop at entertainment. The Action Blueprint calculates your Cognitive Burden shift—dropping stress from 88% down to 18%. It isolates the mental antagonist, offers a grounding reality check, and gives you three 5-minute micro-actions. Checking them off triggers positive reinforcement, and one click copies the plan straight into Notion or Apple Notes."*

---

### Act 4: AI Co-Director & Lens Reframing (1:20 - 1:45)
* **Screen:** Open the right studio panel. Click the **"🏛️ Stoic"** chip, then the **"🎭 Satirical"** chip. Highlight the 3-way context switcher: `🎯 This Page` | `🔮 Next Ep` | `🌐 World & Lore`.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Our persistent AI Co-Director lets you steer the narrative with one-click philosophical lenses. Switch to Stoic to view the problem through Marcus Aurelius's dichotomy of control, or Satirical for therapeutic standup comedy. The three-way context switcher keeps track of this current page, the next episode pitch, and your overarching world bible."*

---

### Act 5: Meta SAM 3.1 Interactive Segmentation (1:45 - 2:05)
* **Screen:** Click the **"👁 SAM 3.1"** pill in the top toolbar. Hover over characters and speech bubbles (highlighting masks). Click directly on a speech bubble, change the dialogue text in the inline card, and hit **"Save Dialogue"**.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Powered by Meta SAM 3.1, our interactive vision overlay segments characters, props, and dialogue balloons in real-time. Want to change what a character says? Simply click the speech bubble directly on the comic page and rewrite it inline with instant canvas synchronization."*

---

### Act 6: Motion Comic Reel & Video Export (2:05 - 2:25)
* **Screen:** Click **"🎬 Motion Reel"**. Show the full-screen modal animating panels with Ken Burns camera zoom, speech bubble reveals, and Web Audio ambient synthesizer drone. Toggle to **9:16 Vertical Reel**, hit **"⏺ Record & Export"**, and show the `.webm` download.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Static comics can be exported as high-res PNGs, or turned into dynamic video reels using Meta Movie Gen cinematography directives. With dynamic Ken Burns tracking, built-in ambient Web Audio synth drones, and instant 9:16 vertical reel export, creators can share their story directly to Instagram Reels or TikTok."*

---

### Act 7: Granular Art Director & Mobile Native UI (2:25 - 2:50)
* **Screen:** Open **"Granular Studio"** to show per-style RAG modifiers (Noir, Manga, 4K Realism). Then resize window to mobile screen width: show the Apple-style bottom nav dock (`✦ AI Chat`, `📖 World`, `☑ Plan`, `🎬 Reel`, `⊞ Controls`).
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Every detail is configurable through our Granular Art Director—from layout slicing to style-specific RAG embeddings. And every feature is accessible on mobile through our Apple Intelligence bottom dock.*  
  > *MindStrip solves a universal human problem by uniting Meta's language, vision, segmentation, and video intelligence into a single consumer-grade platform. Thank you."*
